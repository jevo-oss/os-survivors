What's new · v62

Brand-new HD art. Every hero, monster and boss has been redrawn by hand in a detailed, painted style with proper lighting and armour shine.

Animated heroes. The Warrior, Ranger, Mage and Cleric now breathe while idle, walk with real steps, and swing, draw or cast when they attack.

New monsters and bosses. Chickens, rats, goblins, cows, skeletons, dragons, the Ember Titan and all nine bosses have new artwork.

Loot beams overhauled. Drops now rise in layered pillars of light, coloured by rarity, with glowing rune rings and floating sparks.

New pickups and world. Coins, XP gems, chests, food, projectiles, trees, rocks, ruins and the grass have all been redrawn.

From v61:

Juicier hits. Monsters flash and recoil when you hit them, and critical hitsplats land bigger.

Level-up fanfare. Levelling up now pops with a burst of light and fireworks before you pick your upgrade.

Treasure chests. Chests rattle on the ground, then shake harder and glow before they burst open in a shower of sparks.

Skill fireworks. A little firework goes off over your head whenever a skill levels up mid-run.

Boss arrivals. The ground rumbles when a boss shows up, and boss kills land with a flash.

From v60:

Feedback button. Found a bug or have an idea? Tap Feedback on the main menu to leave a comment on the game page.

From v59:

Class balance. Warrior, Ranger and Mage hit harder and survive better, so they keep pace with the Cleric. The Cleric is still a little stronger than the rest.

Smoother mid-game climb. Monster toughness now follows your account progress more evenly, so each run gets you further without a sudden wall in the middle tiers.

Maxed accounts hit harder. Fully maxed accounts are a bit stronger than before, so all that grinding really pays off.

Weapon parity. Dragon bow and Falcon are toned down a little. Prayer and Quake are buffed so they're worth picking.

Your progress is safe. Gold, skills, perks, class levels and Danger tier progress carry over unchanged.
